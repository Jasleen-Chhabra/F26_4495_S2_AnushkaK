# Inventory and Procurement Decision-Support System
CSIS 4495-002 Applied Research Project, Fall 2026

**Team:** Anushka Kshirsagar, Jasleen Chhabra  **Instructor:** Padmapriya Arasanipalai Kandhadai

## Overview
A browser-based decision-support app that combines sales history, current
stock, incoming shipments and lead times to forecast demand, project future
stock and suggest when and how much to reorder.

## Research question
How can historical demand, current inventory, incoming shipments and product
lead times be combined to improve inventory replenishment decisions?

## Planned stack
Python, pandas, statsmodels, Streamlit, Plotly, SQLite
