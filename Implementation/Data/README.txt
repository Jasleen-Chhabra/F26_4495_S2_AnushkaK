Sample dataset v3 - CSIS 4495 Inventory & Procurement Decision-Support System
Generated by generate_dataset.py (seed 4495). Snapshot date: 2026-10-07.

products.csv         300 SKUs: category, supplier_id, pack_size, lead_time_days, unit_cost, selling_price, warehouses
sales_monthly.csv    Jan 2022 - Sep 2026 (57 months), per SKU & warehouse: units_sold, avg_selling_price,
                     revenue (= units_sold * avg_selling_price), promo_flag (1 = discount month, price 10-30% lower, demand higher)
inventory.csv        Current on-hand quantity per SKU & warehouse
purchase_orders.csv  Open POs (ordered / in_transit / delayed) with ETAs, plus received POs with actual_arrival_date;
                     includes unit_cost and total_cost
inventory.db         The same 4 tables in SQLite

Notes: seasonality (holiday peak Nov-Dec, summer peak May-Aug, flat, mild), ~3% yearly price increase, ~8% promo months,
a few low-sales months. Warehouses: WH1, WH2, WH3. About 30% of SKUs are at shortage risk, 50% fine, 20% excess.
Forecasting ideas: units_sold (moving average, Holt-Winters/SARIMA), revenue, price vs. demand during promos.
